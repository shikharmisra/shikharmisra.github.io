Shikhar Misra

I am a TD Module and Integration Yield Engineer at Intel, where I work on 7nm and 5nm chip process.

I completed my PhD in the school of Materials Engineering at Purdue University, advised by Prof. Haiyan Wang. In the past, I have interned at EPFL, University of Vienna and Hokkaido University. I graduated from Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur with a Bachelors and Masters (Dual degree) in Materials Science and Engineering, where I worked with Dr. Krishanu Biswas.

My research interests include developing new materials design through self-assembled metal-oxide and oxide nanocomposites epitaxial thin films for potential application as metamaterials and multiferroics.

Feel free to contact me regarding anything that interests you on this site.
